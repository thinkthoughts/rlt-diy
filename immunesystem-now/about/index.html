Independent acceptance methodology

Nonfiction intelligence

Four layers of correspondence

Imagination supplies candidate explanations and designs. Nonfiction engineering establishes the relationships and records that support claims about them.

01 · Convention

Register the conditions

Notation, units, definitions, domains, axioms and assumptions establish the conditions under which a claim is read.

02 · Written expression

Preserve the specification

Register the original paper and distinguish its claims from mathematical transcription, engineering requirements and proposed extensions.

03 · Proved relationship

Establish the mathematics

Use analytical derivations, Lean and supported RLT proof fragments. Identify exact statements, dependencies, proof boundaries and counterexamples.

04 · Physical record

Establish correspondence

Retain original measurements, calibration records and experimental provenance. Synthetic records test models; physical records test their correspondence.

DIY methodology

From paper to platform

An author can start with an arXiv paper, a research specification or an existing repository. The workflow is currently a human-directed, AI-assisted research process, not an automated submission service.

01

Register sources

Pin the document version and hash; preserve rights and exact claim locations.

02

Formalize relationships

Write definitions, assumptions, domain restrictions, proofs and counterexamples.

03

Specify engineering

Derive interfaces, algorithms, acceptance criteria and computational invariants.

04

Develop it yourself

Initialize an author-directed repository with versioned prompts, code, tests and pinned dependencies.

05

Verify independently

Audit theorem statements, implementations, reproducibility and source correspondence.

06

Test physical correspondence

Design controlled experiments with independent outcomes, uncertainty and explicit stop conditions.

Generalization rule. Application mathematics stays outside the trusted RLT kernel. Reusable engineering patterns inform DIY; general proof-language extensions require separate justification and review.

Author-directed development

Outreach, invitation and handoff

Independent reference implementations can help an author establish and direct a separate project repository. The handoff includes the source register, mathematical companion, development roadmap, verification commands, open obligations and explicit limitations.

Participation, project ownership, licensing, funding and any product-specific work are agreed with the author. Independent research is not an assertion of affiliation or endorsement.
