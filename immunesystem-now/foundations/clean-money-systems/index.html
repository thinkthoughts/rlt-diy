Monetary accounting, contribution and sustainability

The equality $0.96 + $0.04 = $1.00 establishes monetary arithmetic, while origins, distribution, consent, resource expenditure and consequences require separate specifications and evidence.

Research obligations

  • Specify issuance, attribution, distribution and accounting.
  • Distinguish scientific contributions from monetary participation.
  • Compare incentives and measurable physical outcomes against explicit alternatives.
  • Record uncertainty, constraints and trade-offs; avoid treating monetary value as a sustainability metric.
