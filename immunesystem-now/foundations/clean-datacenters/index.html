FNS-003 — Fractal Neural Synthesis investigates procedural weight generation. Reduced storage alone is insufficient to establish reduced energy consumption. Compare energy per useful inference under equivalent quality and workload, with hardware and measurement boundaries recorded. The preliminary assessment identifies an 8-byte seeded ELM as a relevant constant-storage comparator, alongside dense models. No independent energy results are available. Read the assessment.

Leading specification · proposed

DIY-S1-001 — Resource-to-useful-work correspondence

Specify comparable accepted workloads before measuring. Record time-resolved energy and carbon intensity, allocated embodied emissions, meter scope, uncertainty and missing data.

Operational emissions = sum of interval energy × intensity. Divide operational plus allocated embodied emissions by comparable useful work. Report energy per useful work separately. Hourly carbon-free matching is a separate accounting claim.
