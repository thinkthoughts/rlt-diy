True Intelligence

Attribution is the gate. Viability is the payoff.

An independent RLT-FRO engineering companion to the architecture proposed by Carina Nicolosi, founder of Harmonic Field Technologies LLC. The pilot investigates how shared-clock measurements can distinguish magnetic sources and support subsequent studies of whole-system viability.
