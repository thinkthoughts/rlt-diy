Reduced storage alone is insufficient to establish reduced energy consumption.

The 24-byte seed is distinct from total persistent model storage, working memory, firmware and readout parameters. Procedural generation exchanges storage for computation. Compare equivalent useful work and output quality, memory traffic, iteration costs, latency and measured energy within declared boundaries. Edge results require separate verification before generalization to data centers.

Modular indexing

Coprime strides permute residues within a sub-grid, but adjacent closed cells can share coordinates. At M=3, indices 1 and 2 coincide at the exact normalized point (1/3, −1).

Taylor remainder

Continuous differentiability alone guarantees o(s), rather than necessarily O(s²). The Lean counterexample uses the real-power function with exponent 3/2 at zero, addressing the universal C¹ assertion in the pinned manuscript.

Vogel sampling geometry

The exact centered affine x-field on 256 normalized Vogel samples, reshaped to 16×16, has a leading 3×3 determinant D satisfying 0.001075694 < D < 0.001075695. This establishes exact rank at least three for that affine test matrix, using rational interval arithmetic and explicit analytic bounds rather than a Lean interval theorem.
