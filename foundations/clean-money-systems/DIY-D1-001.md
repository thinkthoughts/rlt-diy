# DIY-D1-001 — Distribution correspondence

Status: proposed leading specification, 2026-11-28. Initial application DSC-004; no operator participation, implementation or acceptance implied.

## Meaning and boundaries

A distribution certificate binds an observed contribution record to a versioned rule and recorded payment. A signature establishes integrity and signer attribution under a trusted key, not the truth of observations or scientific value. Consent, eligibility, fairness and sustainability require separate evidence.

## Inputs and exact arithmetic

Register a batch identifier, time window, network/asset and base-unit scale, canonical serialization version, rule hash, source snapshot hashes, prior batch identity, authorized signer and key lifecycle. Each row uses a batch-scoped participant reference, unique contribution/event identifiers, integer contribution quantities with units, eligibility decision, destination commitment, expected amount, actual amount, and transaction output identifier (txid and output index). Preserve source revisions and corrections; reject duplicate contributions or payments. Specify rounding, allocation remainder, fees, adjustments, exceptions and unpaid obligations explicitly. Never silently equate points with scientific utility.

For each eligible row i, expected amount e_i = rule_v(inputs_i). Require actual attributed amount a_i = e_i, or an explicit separately authorized adjustment and reason. If a payment output is shared, allocate its amount across rows and verify allocation sums; a transaction ID alone is insufficient. Verify batch totals, no double counting across batches, output existence, amount, destination binding, chain identity and declared confirmation/finality policy. Pending, orphaned, disputed and unmatched outputs remain separately classified. No secret keys required by the verifier.

## Evidence and acceptance

- D1-01: canonical signed bytes, key authorization and replay protection.
- D1-02: exact input-to-rule correspondence; complete eligible population or explicitly bounded subset.
- D1-03: deterministic integer calculation and documented rounding.
- D1-04: unique attribution to actual outputs and reconciliation of totals.
- D1-05: correction history, independent review and privacy/consent controls.

Public data should be minimized: no public username-to-address mapping by default. Use consented restricted disclosure or reviewed privacy-preserving commitments; commitments alone cannot establish hidden facts. The verifier distinguishes arithmetic acceptance, signature acceptance, external observation provenance and physical/scientific validation. Test missing rows, duplicate IDs, altered rules, incorrect amounts, reused outputs, wrong network, reorgs and unauthorized signatures. A synthetic fixture is not a real payout audit.
