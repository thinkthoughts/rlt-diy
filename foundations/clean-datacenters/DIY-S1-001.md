# DIY-S1-001 — Resource-to-useful-work correspondence

Status: proposed leading specification, 2026-11-28. Initial applications DSC-004 and potential FNS-003 comparisons. No measured benefit claimed.

## Functional unit and comparison

Define useful work R > 0, workload identity, scientific acceptance or service quality criteria, hardware/software version, execution boundary, region, period and comparator before collection. Compare equivalent accepted workloads and quality; count failed/retried attempts and idle allocation within the declared boundary. Folding work units vary by project and complexity: stratify by project/workload or justify normalization. Points and money are not energy or scientific utility.

## Measurement record

Record run ID, workload hash or approved reference, timestamps and duration, accepted/rejected work, meter identity, calibration and sampling interval, wall energy or explicitly scoped device telemetry, allocation of shared infrastructure, location granularity, time-resolved grid intensity source and methodology, embodied hardware inventory/allocation, uncertainty and missingness. Avoid double counting wall and component power or facility overhead. Account separately for water, cooling, hardware lifecycle and other material impacts. Obtain opt-in consent, minimize participant identifiers and publish aggregates where appropriate.

For time intervals j, E_j in kWh and I_j in gCO2e/kWh, operational emissions O = sum_j E_j I_j. With allocated embodied emissions M in gCO2e, intensity C = (O+M)/R in gCO2e per functional unit. Report E/R separately. Use exact rational arithmetic for recorded quantities with declared rounding; arithmetic precision does not remove measurement uncertainty. For nonnegative interval bounds, sum lower products and upper products; divide lower numerator by upper R and upper numerator by positive lower R. Distinguish estimates from calibrated observations. Report missing terms rather than substituting zero.

## Acceptance

- S1-01: comparable workload and accepted-work definition.
- S1-02: energy measurement, calibration, allocation and completeness.
- S1-03: time/location intensity and embodied-emissions provenance.
- S1-04: unit conversion, arithmetic, uncertainty and reproducible aggregation.
- S1-05: baseline comparison, repeatability and trade-offs.

Hourly local carbon-free matching is a separate procurement/accounting claim; record certificate scope and avoid double counting. It is not automatically equivalent to zero physical emissions. The proposed SCI-style calculation needs standards correspondence review before any conformity claim. Do not assert folding dominates node energy without measurement. No single metric establishes sustainability.

## DSC adapter

Use opt-in comparable Folding@home runs, separately measure node operation, and distinguish contributor hardware from datacenter infrastructure. Pilot collection requires an agreed protocol and consent. No incentives or consensus changes. See https://rlt-fro.now/DSC/.
